Review the enclosed questions before consulting the repository's authored labels. This is a public development corpus of 56 synthetic preference pairs, not an unseen benchmark or live-market observations.

1. Open `QUESTIONS.md` or `blind-review.json`.
2. In `review-template.json`, fill your name/identifier, ISO review date, relationship to the project, and whether you reviewed without seeing source labels.
3. For each case, choose `A`, `B`, `tie`, or `invalid` in `preferred`. Explain the calculation, assumption or evidence behind your choice in `rationale`. Keep IDs and hashes unchanged.
4. Return the completed JSON and any supporting references. If doing a partial review, remove unanswered objects; do not invent choices to fill the template.

The author will compare this review with the frozen authored labels, preserve disagreements and seek corrections where needed. Agreement alone is not objective accuracy. Do not sign wallet messages, pay anything or install software to complete this review.
